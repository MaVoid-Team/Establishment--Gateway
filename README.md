# Liwan Portal

A staff workspace for purchase orders, contracts, maintenance, and the records that keep a company running.

Liwan Portal is for the people who work at Liwan — staff, managers, and admins who need one place to request purchases, send legal work for review, file maintenance issues, and keep contacts and documents in order. It works in English and Arabic.

- Submit a purchase order, send it through approval and signatures, then look it up later or print it.
- Open a maintenance ticket or a legal review request and follow it until it is resolved.
- Keep employees, departments, companies, and vendors — plus their documents — in one directory.
- Sign contracts and other files, and get a heads-up when something is about to expire.
- For leadership: see spend, contract value, and payment analytics without chasing spreadsheets.

## Try it

[Live site](https://establishment-gateway.vercel.app)

## How it works

You sign in and land on a portal of tools. What you can open depends on your role. The app talks to a private API, and live notifications keep people in the loop when orders, tickets, or signatures move.
